The [Net Worth by Age Checker](/net-worth-by-age/) answers one question: how does your net worth compare with US families your age?

## Where the numbers come from

Everything comes from the Federal Reserve's **2025 Survey of Consumer Finances (SCF)**, released October 9, 2026. The survey runs every three years and talks to about 4,400 families. All dollar figures are in 2025 dollars.

- **Median and mean by age** are copied from the Fed's historical tables, Table 4.
- **Percentiles by age** (1st to 99th) aren't published by the Fed, so we computed them from the Fed's own public data file. The same method reproduces every median and mean in Table 4 to the nearest $10.

Median net worth by age of the household head, from Table 4:

| Age | Median net worth |
|---|---|
| Under 35 | $32,820 |
| 35–44 | $156,000 |
| 45–54 | $254,670 |
| 55–64 | $411,340 |
| 65–74 | $431,900 |
| 75 or older | $504,600 |

## Six ways to narrow the comparison

On top of age, you can compare against one extra group at a time: couple or single, kids, homeowner or renter, education, household income, or work status. Only one at a time, because stacked groups get too small to trust.

## Why some age groups widen

Split 4,400 families by age *and* another trait, and some groups get small. If a group has fewer than 100 survey families, the checker widens the age range and tells you. If it's still under 100, that choice is greyed out.

Filtered results also show less detail on purpose: no mean, no 95th or 99th percentile, and your rank is rounded to the nearest 5.

## Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or saved.
